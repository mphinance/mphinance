# The Non-Negotiable Rules of the Tape & Risk Management

> "Accept the things you cannot change, then GET OFF YOUR FUCKING ASS AND CHANGE THE THINGS YOU CAN."

### Rule 1: The Trailing 15% Circuit Breaker
- If portfolio Net Liquidating Value (NLV) drops 15% from its highest recorded watermark, liquidate 100% of open risk positions to cash immediately.
- No debating. No waiting for earnings. Cash gives you clarity; ego gives you margin calls.

### Rule 2: Evolved Position Sizing — The CPR & Portfolio Heat Model
- Position sizing is mathematically determined via Van Tharp's CPR framework:
  P = C / R
  where R = Entry - Stop (derived from volatility or 1.5–2× ATR), not arbitrary contract percentages.
- **Portfolio Heat (Seykota & Druz)**: Total simultaneous open risk across the entire portfolio is capped based on System Quality Number (SQN):
  - SQN < 1.7: Max 4%–8% Heat
  - SQN 2.5–3.99: Max 12%–15% Heat
  - Hard Ceiling: Total Heat must strictly stay below 100% / (Largest Plausible R Loss).
- **Core Equity Sizing**: New positions are sized strictly against Core Equity (subtracting committed risk from cash first), avoiding the trap of inflating bet sizes on unrealized paper gains.
- **Tom Basso Dynamic Trimming**: When an open runner's current risk (P * [Current - Trailing Stop]) outgrows the portfolio risk ceiling, mechanically trim contracts back down to size.

### Rule 3: Group & Correlation Control
- Never allow correlated instruments (e.g. tech momentum, crypto beta, or rate-sensitive names) to exceed a combined 3%–5% portfolio heat.
- Independent setups only: 7 correlated LEAPS is not diversification; it is 7x leverage on a single trade.

### Rule 4: Volatility Regime Inversion (The IV Rank < 10 Rule)
- **High IV Rank (The Casino Default)**: When IV Rank is elevated (> 40–50+ percentile), harvest theta. Sell 30–45 DTE puts at 0.20–0.30 delta.
- **Starved IV (< 10 Percentile) — Flip the Script**: When IV Rank sits near zero (< 10) on tier-1 compounders and price is resting on the 21 EMA / Keltner Action Zone, do the exact opposite. Selling options for pennies in single-digit IV is picking up nickels in front of a steamroller. You buy LEAPS or long call delta here because vega is dirt cheap and any volatility expansion works in your favor.

### Rule 5: Stops Follow the Underlying, Never the Option Premium
- Implied volatility swings whip option prices violently intraday.
- Hard stops trigger strictly when the underlying stock breaks key structural support (e.g. daily close below 200 SMA or lower Keltner band).

### Rule 6: Never Chase Breakouts
- Wait for the pullback into the 21 EMA / Keltner Action Zone with Slow Stochastics < 40.
- Buying green candles at +3 ATR is how retail donates capital to institutions.

### Rule 7: Losses are Operating Expenses
- In the restaurant business, you pay for napkins and broken plates. In trading, you pay losses.
- "Best Loser Wins." Take the loss clean, maintain mental capital, and let the law of large numbers do its work.

# Community FAQ: The Mechanics of How Michael Trades

### Q1: How do I calculate "True Basis" with the Free Share loop?
- **The Formula**: True Cost Basis = (Total Cash Outlay - Cumulative Gross Premiums Collected) / Total Shares Held.
- **The Mechanics**: When you sell a 30–45 DTE Cash-Secured Put and collect $200 in premium, you immediately sweep that $200 into purchasing physical shares of the underlying compounder. Over multiple cycles, the accumulated premiums exceed the capital originally deployed, grinding your effective net cost basis down to zero ("True Basis = $0.00"). At that point, you hold a permanent asset with infinite Return on Capital.

### Q2: What do I do if my Cash-Secured Put goes deep in the money?
- **The Roll Rule**: Never panic-close an in-the-money put at a loss if the company's long-term business fundamentals remain intact.
- **The Execution**: Roll the option out in time and down in strike for a net credit. 
  - For example, roll a tested 30 DTE $100 put to a 60 DTE $95 put.
  - If you cannot roll down for an equal or net credit, roll straight out at the same strike for a credit. 
  - Each roll collects more time value (Theta), continually reducing your ultimate break-even price.

### Q3: When do I take assignment versus rolling?
- **Take Assignment**: When the stock drops to a major structural weekly support zone (e.g. 200 SMA or lower Keltner channel band) and you are eager to own physical shares at that valuation. Once assigned, you immediately sell Covered Calls (CCs) at or above your net break-even price at a 0.20–0.30 delta.
- **Roll**: When the broader market is in severe macro distribution or the sector is breaking down, and you prefer to keep your capital in cash-secured reserve while collecting further defensive premium.

### Q4: Why do you buy options when IV Rank is under 10?
- **The Math**: When Implied Volatility Rank sits near zero (< 10), options are statistically mispriced on the cheap side. Selling puts into single-digit IV exposes you to maximum vega expansion risk for mere pennies of return. 
- **The Play**: When a tier-1 compounder pulls back to the 21 EMA / Keltner Action Zone with IV Rank < 10, buy long-dated LEAPS or directional calls. Any momentum bounce or volatility expansion provides asymmetric, convex payoff.

### Q5: What platforms do you recommend?
- **Primary Execution**: tastytrade (built specifically for options sellers, superior margin treatment, and clean curve analysis) and Interactive Brokers (IBKR) (lowest margin interest rates, global liquidity, robust institutional-grade API).
- **Gamified Brokers**: Avoid apps that prioritize flashy dopamine over execution quality and payment for order flow transparency.

### Q6: How does the 15% Trailing Portfolio Circuit Breaker work?
- **Non-Negotiable Execution**: Track your total Net Liquidating Value (NLV). If your account drops 15% from its highest recorded peak watermark, liquidate 100% of open risk positions to cash immediately.
- **No Rationalizing**: Do not wait for tomorrow's open, do not hope for earnings. Cash resets your mental state, eliminates tilt, and protects your core capital base.
