## Template

**What the paywall reliably hides:**
- **Single-name deep dives:** the quantitative payoff — valuation math/implied price target, technical entry/stop ("Chart and Entry"), position size as a % of the stated 7-figure/30+-name book, and (when relevant) explicit M&A/buyout framing. The free preview over-delivers numbers and narrative (guidance, margins, cap tables, bear case) right up to the "so what do we actually own and how big" line, then cuts.
- **Macro notes:** preview = diagnosis (what's happening, why), paywall = prescription (what trade follows).
- **Portfolio updates:** "What We've Sold" (flattering, retrospective) ships free-ish; "What We've Added" and the current-holdings/weightings table are always walled — they'll tell you what worked, not what they're doing right now.

**How hard to decode:** Easy-to-medium. This author over-discloses in the free section relative to most paywalled finance Substacks — specific entry prices, cap tables, guidance figures, and full table-of-contents headers are all visible — so paid-content *categories* can be inferred with Medium–High confidence from preview + TOC alone. The genuinely hidden pieces are the numbers that turn thesis into action: price targets, stop levels, and position sizing.
