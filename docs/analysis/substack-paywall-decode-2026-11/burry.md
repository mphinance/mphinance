## Template

What the paywall reliably hides, across all five: **the specific number** — exact IV15/fair-value targets, trade tickets (what he bought/sold, at what price, how much he trimmed), and position sizing. The free preview almost always gives you the thesis and the setup (the news, the math framework, the question) and cuts off right before the number that turns it into an actionable call. The shorter the paywalled chunk (Trading Post Nov 5, Nov 6), the more likely it's a pure trade confirmation bolted onto an already-complete free thesis; the longer the paywalled chunk (Anthropic S-1/A, Portpourri), the more likely it's a full secondary analysis or a multi-position rundown withheld almost entirely.

Decoding difficulty: **Medium**. Burry's posts get heavy, detailed secondary press coverage within 24–72 hours (StockTwits, 24/7 Wall St, Yahoo Finance, regional outlets), which reliably recovers the *content* (tickers, directional calls, rough price levels) even though no outlet quotes his paywalled text verbatim. The newest post (Portpourri, 1 day old) was the one case with zero press coverage yet — freshness, not the paywall itself, is the binding constraint there.
