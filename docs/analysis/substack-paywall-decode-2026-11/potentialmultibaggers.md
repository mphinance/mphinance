## Template
Two distinct paywall shapes in this publication:
1. **Single-stock earnings deep-dives (4x/week):** Free preview = "The Numbers" (full GAAP/non-GAAP metrics table, always ungated) + one headline narrative angle. Gated = Selling Rules checklist, updated Quality Score, full DCF/multiple-based valuation, and an explicit Buy/Hold/Sell verdict. This is the easiest paywall in the batch to decode — the author literally lists what's behind it as the subscribe hook, every single time, and the free section's tone (bullish/defensive framing) telegraphs which way the verdict leans even without the number.
2. **Sunday "Overview of the Week":** Free preview = full macro commentary and all numbered "Quick Facts." Gated = the Forever Portfolio ledger (weights, trims/adds) and probably any additional quick facts/recap not shown. No explicit teaser is given for these, so confidence is lower — this is inference from his own /about page template, not from the post's own promise.

**Difficulty:** Easy for the 3 stock deep-dives (author hands you the paywall's table of contents and the directional tilt in the free text); harder for the 2 weekly roundups, where you're reconstructing the gated section from his stated recurring-feature list rather than from anything in that specific post.
