The 11 gaps, and why

Broker execution you cannot reach today

This is the whole story. You have the directory (awesome-broker-mcp) and an adapter theory (traderdaddy-bridge maps brokers into Tradier's shape), but the execution paths you can actually drive are Tradier and Vesper's keyless Webull. Five majors are open:

  1. k1xme/schwab-mcp (0 stars, no license) - Schwab, 18 tools including option chains, order list, preview, place, cancel, replace, plus a Claude Code plugin of six safety skills. Schwab is the major one you have no route to at all.
  2. tomasgesino/schwab-mcp (4 stars, MIT) - the wheel-specific Schwab server: sell_cash_secured_put and sell_covered_call with dry_run=true by default. This is your wheel bias expressed as a tool.
  3. sblattj/etrade-mcp (1 star, MIT) - E*TRADE with OAuth 1.0a, VIP-TOTP auto-renew, equity, options and multi-leg spreads behind a code-enforced preview-then-place handshake.
  4. jonathansudhakar1/tradestation-cli-mcp (0 stars, MIT) - all 42 TradeStation v3 REST endpoints as a library, a CLI and an MCP server, with streaming for stocks, options and futures.
  5. tastytrade/tastytrade-mcp (37 stars, official) - the official one: quotes, chains, balances, positions, order entry, dry-run gated, read-only env switch, environment tag on every order result. Matches your premium-selling wheel bias directly. cmike444/tastytrade-mcp (2 stars) is the fuller sibling at 72 tools with DXLink realtime and option greeks.
  6. a9lim/tasty-mcp (0 stars, AGPL-3.0) - the interesting one. Its write tools are gated by a hard cap on total potential max-loss across positions and working orders (default $100), it rejects short equity and naked short calls, and it always allows closing orders. That is an exposure-bounded order gate for a small account selling premium, which is exactly your stated account shape.
  7. alpacahq/alpaca-mcp-server (1,102 stars, MIT) - Alpaca's official server, stocks, ETFs, crypto and options plus market data and portfolio, v2 FastMCP rewrite.
  8. webull-inc/webull-openapi-mcp (31 stars, Apache-2.0) - the official Webull MCP. Vesper is Webull oriented but keyless by design; this is the order-capable one.
  9. webull-inc/webull-openapi-cli (7 stars, Apache-2.0) - the Go CLI companion: dry-run, order confirmation, OAuth device auth, structured JSON output. Agent-first.

The one that is not a broker

  1. mnemox-ai/tradememory-protocol (1,450 stars, MIT) - local-first memory and a risk brake for AI trading agents: it syncs your fills, surfaces where your own history loses money, and can refuse orders that break your rules before they reach the broker (20 MCP tools, Alpaca sync shown read-only). Nothing in your stack does outcome-based trade memory. This is the most interesting non-obvious find in the whole set.

What you already cover (so you do not re-buy it)

  • thammo4/uvatradier, fi222/tradier-mcp, pipeworx-io/mcp-tradier - all Tradier. You already normalize into Tradier through traderdaddy-bridge and pinesnake.
  • nutdnuy/QuantCornerBacktestingWebullMCP - backtest and validation skills that alpha-skills covers.
  • Your own repos, correctly identified: momentum-mcp, alpha-skills, trading-agent, traderdaddy-sdk.

What I would do

  1. Take the three wheel-shaped ones seriously: tastytrade/tastytrade-mcp (official, dry-run gated), a9lim/tasty-mcp (the max-loss gate is a genuinely good idea worth copying even if you do not run it), and tomasgesino/schwab-mcp (CSP and CC entry with dry-run default).
  2. Read mnemox-ai/tradememory-protocol end to end - the pre-order refusal driven by your own losing history is the one idea here that is not already in your stack in any form.
  3. Treat the rest as a map, not a shopping list. You have the directory; these are implementations. The value is knowing which broker has an official MCP with real order-gating, not adopting eleven servers.

Caveats, stated plainly

  • Grading was done in eight passes by separate workers against one stack description, so it is consistent in vocabulary, not perfectly calibrated. The 11 gaps I verified by hand against the GitHub API (stars, license, last push, archived all match). The 91 adjacent and 136 noise I did not individually re-check.
  • Stars are not quality. Several of the most interesting gaps (a9lim, k1xme, jonathansudhakar1, cmike444) have 0 to 4 stars.
  • Two of the strongest gaps carry no license at all (k1xme/schwab-mcp, cmike444/tastytrade-mcp), which means all rights reserved. Read, do not vendor.
