How to read it

A fraction is levels that closed on the right side, out of levels price actually reached. The $5 column scores the same number of plain round numbers on each side of spot, by the same rule. Better compares the two hit rates for that session.

6 sessions scored nothing for the map. On 4 it named levels price never reached. On 2 every level it named was a kind it reports but does not score, such as a put wall. The round numbers still scored on 2 of those 6. The overall rate cannot see these sessions, which is why they stay in the table.

Where dealers are long gamma they brake, where they are short they chase. Not advice.
