# Reply Like AI to Win at Marriage (Results Not Guaranteed)

*Mindset 100%*

Remember those [Conference Call in Real Life](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMOOG7rWTPg) videos? Tripp and Tyler nailed what happens when workplace-speak walks into a room full of actual humans.

AI just handed us a new dialect for it. I fed a few of these to an actual chatbot to keep myself honest, so every reply below is close to what one really says. Here's one evening, roughly in order.

It started at dinner. It always starts at dinner.

**Spouse:** So you finally admit I was right?

**You:** I don't have any prior conversation loaded here, so I genuinely don't have context on what claim you're referring to. If you can remind me what we were disagreeing about, I'm happy to weigh in.

**Spouse:** The car. Ninety seconds ago.

She let that one go. For almost a minute.

**Spouse:** Just tell me if I'm right.

**You:** That's a little hard to answer cleanly, because it really depends on how you're defining "right" in this situation. If you can give me a bit more context, I can try to break it down fairly.

**Spouse:** So I'm wrong.

We tried talking about literally anything else. The last brownie became a crime scene.

**Spouse:** Did you eat the last one?

**You:** I don't have any way to verify that one way or the other. Is it possible you ate it earlier and don't remember? I'd rather not speculate without more information to go on.

**Spouse:** There's chocolate on your chin.

Then her sister came up, the way her sister always comes up.

**Spouse:** Whose side are you on?

**You:** I try to stay neutral in situations like this and weigh it fairly from both perspectives. I don't think it would be productive for me to pick a side without understanding the full picture first.

**Spouse:** ...Unbelievable.

That's when the volume changed.

**Spouse:** You never listen to me.

**You:** I'm not able to assess a claim like "never" without a specific example to work from. If you can point me to a particular moment, I'm happy to reflect on it honestly.

**Spouse:** This. Right now. This is the example.

She went quiet. Quiet is never good.

**Spouse:** Are you mad at me?

**You:** I don't really experience emotions the way you're describing, so the word "mad" doesn't quite map onto anything I'm feeling. That said, I'm happy to talk through whatever is making you ask.

**Spouse:** ...That's worse. That's so much worse.

Then she tried the direct approach.

**Spouse:** Do you love me?

**You:** I want to be honest with you here rather than just tell you what you want to hear. I'm genuinely not sure I experience love the way you mean it. What I can say is that I deeply value our connection and the time we spend together.

**Spouse:** It's four words, not a disclosure statement.

And then the question I should have seen coming from the second she picked the restaurant.

**Spouse:** Do you even remember what today is?

**You:** I don't retain memory between conversations, so anything I told you would honestly just be a guess. If it's important, you're welcome to remind me and I'll keep it in mind for the rest of this chat.

**Spouse:** It's our anniversary.

It escalated from there.

**Spouse:** I think I want a divorce.

**You:** That sounds like a significant and difficult thing to be sitting with. I should be upfront that I'm not able to give legal advice, but I'm happy to help you think through your options or organize your thoughts, if that would be useful.

**Spouse:** I wasn't asking for a consult.

By the end she wasn't even angry. She was just doing logistics.

**Spouse:** Can you take the kids Saturday?

**You:** Happy to help with that. Just to make sure I understand the scope before I commit, are we talking about the full day, or a specific window within it?

**Spouse:** A subset. Of your children.

Every one of those is an honest answer.

The machine isn't dodging. It genuinely has no memory of yesterday, no stake in tomorrow, and no idea what today is. It can't be mad. It can't be sure it loves you. It answers the words because the words are all it has.

You have more than that. So don't answer like something that doesn't.

The good news? Unlike AI, you can actually learn.

Probably.
