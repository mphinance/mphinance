# I Asked Sam Why the Rotation Feels Weekly

*Macro 50% | Trading 30% | AI 20%*

![One see-saw, two clusters](hero.png)

**Leadership on this market hands off about once a week, and it has been quietly driving me insane.** One week Space and uranium are ripping. The next it's oil and defense. Blink and Palantir is carrying the whole tape by itself.

I got tired of guessing which theme was "in." So I stopped guessing. I pointed Sam, the engine I build all my tooling around, at 385 days of daily bars. 41 tickers, 8 themes I actually trade: data centers, energy, uranium, space, defense, defense-tech, semis. Every name has full history over the window, so nothing is skewed by a fresh listing.

One question. Why does this feel like it turns every week?

Sam's answer was cleaner than I expected.

## It's one see-saw

The whiplash is not eight themes doing eight things. It is two clusters doing one thing, which is trading against each other.

There's a fast growth cluster: semis, data centers, uranium, space, and Palantir-style defense-tech. And there's a slow cluster: energy and old-line defense. When money leaves one, it shows up in the other. Same day. Every time.

You can't see it in raw correlation, because everything shares market beta and everything reads positive. The see-saw only shows up once you strip SPY out and look at each theme's return above or below the market itself.

The signal isn't in the correlations. It's in the correlations after you subtract the index.

![Excess-return correlation matrix, full period](corr_matrix.png)

Read the red first. Semis against defense sits at -0.41. Semis against energy at -0.28. Data centers against energy, data centers against defense, both negative. That is the see-saw, and it's the cleanest structure on the board.

Now read the green. The growth cluster isn't five trades, it's one. Semis and data centers move together at +0.67. Uranium and space at +0.55. If you own three of those names thinking you're diversified, you own the same trade three times.

## The two clocks

The reason it feels weekly is that only one side of the beam is fast.

![Swing cadence by theme](cadence.png)

The growth cluster turns every one to three weeks. Space is unhinged: it ran +98% from late April to late May, then gave back 53% by late June. Eight weeks, full round trip.

Energy and defense move five to ten times slower. They only lurch on something real. An OPEC headline. A ceasefire. A budget. That's why the war and ceasefire news feels like it drives those two, while the AI names churn on their own clock no matter what the front page says.

## Two things I'd write on the wall

**You can't front-run one theme with another.** Every cross-correlation peaks at zero lag. The rotation is same-day. Energy doesn't move Monday so defense follows Tuesday. There is no lead and lag to trade here. You only get to read which cluster is currently bid, then position with it.

**Defense-tech is a bridge, not a defense play.** Palantir, Kratos, AVAV correlate with both the growth cluster at +0.50 and old defense at +0.34. It rides risk-on with the AI names and catches the geopolitics bid too. That is why it feels like it always works, right up until the whole growth cluster gets crushed and the bridge goes down with the ship.

## What I don't know yet

Now the honest part. Some of this is solid. Some of it I'm still watching.

Solid: the see-saw exists, the growth cluster is one trade, and there's no lead or lag to exploit. I will defend all three.

Open: energy against semis went from -0.28 over the full window to -0.36 in the recent half. Is that the rotation growing a real second engine, value grinding higher while growth cools off? Or is it a stretched rubber band about to snap back the other way? I don't know yet. I'm treating -0.36 as the single number to watch. If it keeps widening, the rotation has legs.

And the bridge. What actually breaks the defense-tech link? A Palantir-specific blowup, a defense budget shock, a growth washout that drags it down anyway? I have the correlation. I don't have the cause.

So here's the invite. This is my data run and my read, nothing more. If you've got your own bars, run the SPY-stripped version on your themes and tell me where I'm wrong. Push on the -0.36. Tell me what snaps the bridge. That is the whole reason I put this out for free instead of behind the wall.
