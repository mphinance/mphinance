## (b) New findings

- **Tenant is CoreWeave, Inc. (parent)**, lease dated 8/8/25 (landlord Galaxy Power LLC, assigned to the project sub), amended 1/21/26 and 6/3/26. No LC, no security deposit, no parent-of-parent guarantee disclosed anywhere. Credit support that exists: **$150M prepaid rent** on Galaxy's balance sheet (deferred lease revenue, unrecognized) and a **$100M options payment** per the deck (unclear if the same cash); a **Data Center Lease Termination Fee** exists (amount UNVERIFIED — the lease is not filed) and must be used to repurchase notes at par. CRWV says it uses LCs for "several" DC providers; not identifiable to Helios.
- **Indenture pricing of the CRWV risk is milder than the equity market's**: a lease termination is *not* an EoD if Galaxy signs an LOI for a "Qualifying Tenant" within 6 months and a lease within 12 (Qualifying = NVDA/AAPL/MSFT/AMZN/GOOGL/META affiliates, $50B+ cap listed cloud/tech, or IG-backstopped). Notes also carry a par redemption right on termination. Tells you lenders at 9.875% underwrote re-leasability of the site, and that the whole 1.6 GW is power-approved.
- **Project equity cash flow is back-ended**: deck shows post-debt-service cash of only $4–122M/yr through 2040 on $565M→$830M rent; 4%/yr amort, notes **mature Aug 2031 with ~$3.0B outstanding** → refinancing event. A DCF of unlevered project cash flows minus debt is still the right frame, but the "Helios equity" is an option on refinancing, not a dividend stream.
- **Phase I economics** are the only ones the Q3 print will show: $80M/qtr rent, >90% project EBITDA, from Q3. Q2 lease revenue $18.9M (ramp).
- **Phase III and the 830 MW** are, per the 10-Q, inside the same ERCOT-approved 1.6 GW; the Abbott audit (directive 8/3; PUCT good-cause exceptions 8/20; Batch Zero conditional classifications 9/8 for Helios III 1,000 MW, Selene 900, Caspian 700) touches the *new* 4.2 GW of requests. No filing says the approved 1.6 GW is at risk (UNVERIFIED either way).
