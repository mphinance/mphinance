    3. Extreme Volatility: A Beta of 2.52 means BKSY can (and does, see today) drop precipitously on bad news. While this aids the bull case on the upside, it's a double-edged sword that can destroy capital quickly if the turnaround fails to materialize.
    4. Technical Breakdown: Despite potential oversold conditions, if BKSY fails to hold key support levels, there's a long way down. The 52-week range extends down to $16.34, meaning another 50% drop is entirely within the realm of possibility if the current downtrend accelerates.

    This stock is for aggressive traders only, given the high beta and mixed signals.

Set a tight stop below $30.00 to manage risk, targeting a move back to PP ($34.64) and then R1 ($37.31). This is the play if you believe the market is overreacting.
