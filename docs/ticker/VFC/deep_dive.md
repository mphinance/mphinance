A mere 1.0% revenue growth is easy to beat with focused execution and renewed marketing efforts, driving significant operating leverage from that 5.5% profit margin.
2.  **Forward P/E Implies Recovery:** The massive drop from a TTM P/E of 27.56 to a Forward P/E of 12.72 is a loud signal. The market is anticipating a substantial rebound in earnings. If VFC delivers even partially on these expectations, the stock could re-rate significantly higher.

Continued strategic moves, particularly around inventory management and supply chain efficiency, can dramatically improve profitability and cash flow, making VFC a more attractive "quality-factor" holding despite its current Graham valuation.
4.  **Technical Momentum Shift:** Despite some lingering bearishness, VFC shows clear signs of a technical shift. 2.  **Graham Valuation Disconnect:** While quality factors can justify a premium, the fundamental "OVERVALUED" stamp with a $10.84 intrinsic target cannot be ignored.
