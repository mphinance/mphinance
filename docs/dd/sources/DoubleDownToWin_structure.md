## His 10 sections, in order (each opens with an italic one-line "why this matters")
0. Intro: length warning, why he's qualified, which example ticker, numbered list of sections, source window (last 6 months), inline sources.
   One-line pitch: "${COMPANY} (${TICKER}) is a ${what}. If you're bullish on ${theme}, this could be your jam."
1. Company Fundamentals: what it does (segments) | who runs it (CEO/CIO bios with LinkedIn) | finances (latest quarter, TTM revenue vs net income, cash, debt/equity, key holdings) | Source
2. Recent News (last 6 months): [event, date] + 2-3 line takeaway + Source (earnings, partnerships, listings, legal/regulatory)
3. Growth Drivers: each driver with numbers | Sources
4. Risks and Challenges: market risk | execution/financing risk | operational/security risk | Source each
5. Industry/Competition: per competitor, where they win and where the ticker wins | Source
6. Sentiment: analyst count, consensus, avg and top target | retail chatter (named handles) | Sources
7. Chart Review: YTD, ATH, RSI, 50/200 MA position, support, resistance, trend structure (HH/HL) | chart image
8. Sector and Macro: sector trend | benchmark performance table (1w/1m/3m/6m/YTD/1y) | Sources
9. Valuation and Thesis: market cap, fwd P/E, debt/assets, gross profit, margin, net cash flow, ownership split (institutional/insider/other) | thesis 2-3 sentences | target, timeframe, % upside | labeled as opinion
10. Action Plan and Risk Management: entry level | allocation cap (% of portfolio) | hold period | what to watch | stop % | exit triggers (fundamental) | take profit
Closing: sections 9-10 are the reader's own call; form your own conviction; ask readers what he missed.

## What it does NOT have (from the capture's own notes)
insider transactions as a step, options flow / OI, dark pool, a dedicated SEC filing read (10-K/10-Q/8-K), a catalyst calendar as its own section.

## Style
Long, conversational, emoji section numerals (1️⃣..🔟), bold sub-heads, a *Source:* line with raw URL after each block, opinions labeled as opinions.
