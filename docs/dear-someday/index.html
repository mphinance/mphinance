Where this came from

It started with a post about being someone worth looking up to.

A friend posted four words that are hard to argue with: "Be the person that younger you would look up to." The reply wasn't more words — it was an idea: record something now, so a kid could stumble across it in twenty years. This tool is that reply, actually built.

Be the person that younger you would look up to. — the original post

Most of us reply to advice like that with good intentions and nothing else. Dear Someday exists so the reply doesn't stop at the intention. Write it while you mean it. Pick when it lands. Let time do the delivering.

How it works

STEP 1

Write

Say the thing plainly — typed, or as a short voice memo if that's how it actually comes out. No performance. Just the truth as of right now.

STEP 2

Seal

Choose when it should land: a milestone, or a plain number of years from now. Once it's sealed, nobody — including you — can peek.

STEP 3

Wait

The capsule sits untouched until its date arrives. Then whoever it's addressed to finally gets to open it.

About this preview: in the full product, a sealed capsule delivers itself as a private link on the date you chose — to an inbox, not a browser tab. This demo seals everything right here in your device's storage (text and any attached voice memo alike), so you can try the whole arc — write, seal, wait, open — in the next two minutes instead of twenty years.
