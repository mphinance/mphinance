Read the regime

The two-question read

Run these two checks before you place a single trade. One tells you which game you're playing. The other tells you how safe it is to play it. Everything else is downstream.

1
Which regime?  · the sign of net gamma
POSITIVE GEX · leash on
Dealers are forced to fade every move: buy the dip, sell the rip. Moves get dampened. Calm tape, tight ranges, price pinned toward the big strikes.
Your playSell premium. Fade the walls. The covered-call day.
NEGATIVE GEX · leash off
Dealers are forced to chase every move: sell weakness, buy strength. Moves get amplified. Trending tape, gaps, momentum that runs further than it should.
Your playBuy momentum. Trade with the break. The trend day.
2
How much rope?  · distance from price to the gamma flip
THE FLIP
Well above the flip
Lots of rope. The regime is stable. Trust it and trade the plan above.
Right at the flip
Rope's running out. One catalyst flips calm into chaos. Size down, don't marry the thesis.
Below the flip
You're already in the amplifying regime. Calm-day tactics will get run over.
Remember
The sign tells you which game. The flip tells you how safe. Re-read both every morning. Yesterday's regime is already gone.
