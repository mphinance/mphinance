## The actual point

None of these formats is the smart one. The smart move is figuring out which one you'll actually use, then using it every day until the exam stops scaring you.

That's the whole secret to passing anything, by the way. Not intensity. Frequency. Show up at the material a little bit, daily, and let the reps do what reps do. I learned that lesson the hard way in rooms that had nothing to do with finance, and it turns out it works on a licensing exam too.
