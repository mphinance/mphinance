## Thesis / spine line
> The 200-day tells you the trend. The pullback tells you the entry. The catalyst only counts if it isn't already priced.

The mistake: **buying a chart instead of a location on a chart.** Three green-near-the-highs names can look identical and be three different trades. The filter that separates them = three questions, asked in order. Fail one → watch, don't buy.

## The framework (teach this — it's the free value)

**Q1 — Which side of the 200-day, and which way is it sloping?**
The 200DMA is the line where institutions decide to defend or distribute. Above a *rising* 200 = uptrend (buy pullbacks). Below a *falling* 200 = downtrend (distrust rallies until the line is reclaimed). Slope matters as much as price-vs-line.

**Q2 — At support, or at resistance?**
Oversold at support (rising MA / put-wall shelf, low RSI) = sellers exhausted, risk defined (stop under the shelf). Overbought at resistance (RSI 70+, into a call wall) = you're buying from the people about to sell. The gift vs the trap.

**Q3 — Is the catalyst already priced?**
A catalyst helps only if the tape hasn't reacted. Spent = everyone sees it + already ripped on big volume + overbought (you're exit liquidity). Live = it's dated and real but the tape is asleep (oversold, "falling knife"). Buy the rumor nobody's buying yet.

**Checklist:** rising 200? → at support? → catalyst un-priced? Three yeses = trade. Any no = watch.

## CASE 1 — DIS · fails Q1 (wrong side of the line)  *(FREE)*
**Tape:** $103.89, +3.0%. RSI 57, ADX 36. **EMA stack bearish.** BELOW a declining 200DMA ($106.86). StochK 94 (stretched at the lid).
**Structure:** ascending coil, higher lows (92.19 on 3/27 → 97.95 on 6/10) pressing into a flat lid ~$105–107 = declining 200DMA + $105 gamma wall + Keltner upper, all stacked.
**Teaching point:** a coil into resistance *below a falling 200* is a downtrend trying to reverse, not a reversal. You don't buy the lid. **Graduates to a buy only on** a daily close above $105–107 (reclaims the line). Floor/stop-think $100.

## CASE 2 — RSI (Rush Street Interactive) · passes Q1, fails Q2 & Q3  *(FREE)*
**Tape:** $29.09, +2.9%. **RSI 74 (overbought).** Full bullish stack, **+38% above a RISING 200DMA ($21)** — trend is real (passes Q1).
**The tell:** 6/18 volume = **18.9M, 8.9× the 20-day avg** — a detonation.
**Catalyst:** **S&P SmallCap 600 inclusion, effective 6/22** + $100M buyback + insider secondary. That 8.9× bar = index funds front-running the add.
**Teaching point:** fails Q2 (overbought into the $30 call wall) and Q3 (the catalyst is the headline and already moved it — buy-the-rumor). Index-add pops routinely fade after the effective date. Thin options → shares only. **Better entry:** post-inclusion pullback to $27–28. *Sets up the paid pick: same catalyst, opposite situation.*
