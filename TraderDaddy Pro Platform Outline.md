    \* Market Intel: Broader market research and macro data. (Located in the top menu)

    \* Quality Metrics: Market Depth Rating and Tradability Score.

    \* Data Roster: The list ranking top contracts by Liquidity Score.  
      
    4\. Footer Navigation  
    \* Links to Pricing, Affiliate Program, Support, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service.  
Main Navigation Links & Dropdowns  
      
    1\. Options Flow  
    \* What it is: The live, real-time feed of institutional options trades and unusual options activity as they hit the tape. This is the core 'ticker tape' of the platform.  
      
    2\. Ticker Search  
    \* What it is: The dedicated search engine for looking up options flow, historical data, and specific contract details for an individual stock ticker.  
      
    3\. Top Plays  
    \* What it is: A curated section highlighting high-conviction trade setups, often generated algorithmically based on unusual or highly urgent options activity.  
      
    4\. Flow Analysis (Dropdown Menu)  
    \* Heat Map: Ranks individual stock tickers based on how urgently institutions are accumulating options positions.  
    \* Sector Flow: Tracks where the big money is moving on a macro level, showing options premium flowing in and out of specific market sectors.  
    \* Sector Analysis: Provides a deeper dive into the performance, trends, and specific options positioning within those broader market sectors.  
    \* GEX Calculator: A Gamma Exposure tool used to estimate market maker positioning, which helps traders identify key support, resistance, and potential volatility levels.  
    \* Earnings Flow Scanner: A specialized scanner that isolates options activity specifically for companies with upcoming earnings reports.  
      
    5\. Screeners  
    \* What it is: Custom filtering tools that allow users to build their own specific scans. You can use this to filter the massive daily options flow down to exactly the types of trades you are looking for.  
      
    6\. Smart Tools (Dropdown Menu)  
    \* Watchlist & AI Alerts: Tools to track your favorite assets and set up automated, algorithm-driven notifications for when unusual activity occurs.  
    \* ETF Tracker: Monitors the performance and flow of sector and broad market index funds.  
    \* Liquidity Map: A visual tool to find the most tradable options contracts for a specific ticker based on volume and tight spreads.  
    \* CBOE Options Scanner: Advanced, specialized scanning capabilities pulling directly from the CBOE exchange.  
      
    7\. Market Intel  
    \* What it is: The hub for broader market research, breaking financial news, and macroeconomic data that helps traders understand the overall market environment.
