# The brief behind the kiddo interview

This is a shareable summary of the request given to Codex, Claude, and Gemini. It is not a verbatim transcript of the original request.

Adapt the adult voice interview in `substack-post.md` and `interview-prompt.md` for a child around eight years old. Help the child talk about their world and anything bothering them, without assuming a particular problem or trying to extract a confession.

Deliver a ready-to-use conversation prompt, a short parent setup note, and a brief explanation of the changes. Leave the article for a separate writing pass.

Requirements:

- Use short, concrete questions and familiar interests to begin.
- Ask one question at a time and leave room for the whole answer.
- Accept silence, “I don’t know,” changing the subject, passing, and stopping.
- Gently explore school, friends, home, bedtime, worries, anger, body feelings, and what helps, following the child’s lead.
- Ask what happened before a difficult moment instead of demanding an explanation for behavior.
- Avoid suggesting answers, grading disclosures, assigning blame, diagnosing, or assuming scary games caused a problem.
- Allow pretend play without interpreting it as factual disclosure.
- Never promise secrecy, encourage dependence on AI, or replace trusted adults or professional support.
- Pause for human help if the child mentions harm or danger. A different trusted adult must be an option if the nearby adult is unsafe.
- Offer a brief, correctable reflection only if the child wants one. Stop immediately when asked.
- Let comfort and interest set the length; drop the adult version’s 30-minute target.
- Make the output general enough to share with other parents.

## Product check added during review

Writing a prompt does not establish permission to use it on a particular service. Verify the product’s age rules, supported devices, privacy controls, and actual instruction-following behavior before describing it as a working child-facing setup. A parent can also use the questions directly, without an AI.
