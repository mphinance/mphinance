## How to read this

- **j(given, shipped)** — token Jaccard between the machine's draft and the version Michael shipped. A higher number means the machine was already close; a lower number means his edit budget was large.
- **j(predicted, shipped)** — same, but for the prediction made by running `given.md` through `predict.py` with `/VOICE-DELTA.md` rules applied. **This is the accuracy metric.**
- **Δ** — `j(predicted, shipped) − j(given, shipped)`. Positive means VOICE-DELTA.md moved the draft closer to what Michael actually shipped. Negative means the rules hurt.
- **Rule hits** — did each VOICE-DELTA.md rule actually fire in this pair's diff? `✓` yes, `·` no.
