Haven't posted many trades lately. That's on purpose.

VIX was 15 in August. It's 31 this morning.

The spike isn't what's keeping me out. The floor is. Every time VIX calmed down since September, it calmed down to a higher number: 14.2, 14.8, 16.6, 17.5. And every SPY bounce topped lower: 779, 772, 767, 760. Yesterday it closed at 716.

VVIX closed at 127. When VIX runs and VVIX runs with it, the fear is real. It's not a one-day hiccup.

Then look at the daily ranges. In August QQQ moved about 1.5% a day. Now it's 3.1%. That's what QLD, the 2x, was doing in August. TQQQ went from 4.4% a day to 10%. SPY went from 0.8% to 2%.

So if you need action, stick to plain stocks or a 2x at most. The stocks are already swinging like leveraged ETFs on their own. Put 3x on top of that and you aren't trading anymore, you're buying a lottery ticket.

I'm a wheeler. Fat put premiums look like free money right now. They're fat because the market expects a move bigger than your strike. Sell puts while that floor keeps rising and you end up owning stuff at prices you'll be explaining for months.

So I'm waiting. When VIX finally makes a lower low, I go back to selling. Until then I'm sitting on my hands, and that's a trade too.
