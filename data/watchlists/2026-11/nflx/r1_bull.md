# NFLX Round 1 — BULL case (technicals / price structure / options flow)
Data as of Fri 10/30/2026 close (yfinance daily bars, 3yr history) + TDPro live pull (gamma/bounce score timestamped 11/1 02:25 UTC, i.e. Friday's settled options data).

## Is Friday's relative strength + 4-session hold at $62 seller exhaustion? Short answer: partially — it's a real basing signature, not a confirmed divergence bottom.

**What's real:**
- **10/21 was a genuine volume climax.** 101.4M shares traded (2.92x the 50-day average) — the **97.9th percentile** of every daily volume reading in the last 3 years. That's the day NFLX air-pocketed from 68.69 to 65.04 (-5.3%) absorbing the post-earnings guide-cut selling. Volume since has normalized and trended down on a closing basis (58.7M→37.9M→54.4M→48.8M→41.9M→45.3M→52.3M) — no second climax, no expanding-volume breakdown. Supply looks like it's drying up, not reloading.
- **A higher intraday low formed into Friday.** 10/29 printed the 52-week low (61.39 intraday) but closed at 62.05, +1% off the low. 10/30's low was 61.64 — already higher — and it closed 62.08, +0.05% while SPX fell -1.8% and Nasdaq -2.9% on the Intel/AI-capex scare. That's real relative strength on the ugliest macro tape of the month, not a low-beta coincidence alone (NFLX's beta to SPX is only 0.22, correlation 0.07 — see concession below).
- **This is a rare statistical extension.** NFLX closed Friday **23.9% below its 200dma** — more stretched than 95% of trading days over the last 3 years. The only deeper extension in that window was the Feb 2026 washout (-32% at the extreme), which was followed by some of the best forward returns in the whole dataset (the RSI<32 crosses around 2/19–2/23/2026 ran +19% to +33% over the next 6 weeks). Rare extensions in this name have historically resolved up, eventually.
- **10/28 printed a near-doji right before the low.** Body was only ~4% of the day's range with wicks on both sides, on the close (61.99) immediately preceding the 52-week-low print. Indecision candles clustering at a multi-month low is a textbook (if not definitive) exhaustion tell.
- **The GEX structure and the chart's falling trendline point at the same level.** TDPro's live pull confirms the dossier: gamma flip $65.97, $63 and $65 are the nearest overhead resistance strikes (call-heavy OI), $60 is the dominant put wall/support. The weekly swing-high trendline (83.6 Sep 4 → 73.1 Oct 16) independently projects to ~68-69 this week. A close above ~$66 clears the gamma flip, flips dealers from amplifying to dampening moves, and removes the mechanical tailwind that's been compounding the slide. Small corroborating tell: TDPro's bounce composite improved from -4 yesterday to -3 today — still bearish-leaning, but easing, even as RSI/Stoch/Williams%R all sit at fresh oversold extremes (room to run before overbought).

**Base rate, done honestly (NFLX's own history, not a generic playbook):** NFLX has crossed below RSI 32 about 21 separate times in 3 years. Forward returns from that signal: **5d mean +1.9%/median -0.6% (48% win), 10d +3.3%/0.0% (52%), 21d +2.5%/median -3.8% (45%), 42d mean +6.5%/median +8.9% (60% win).** Translation: the first 2-3 weeks after an RSI-30 cross in this name are a genuine coin flip and the median path is actually flat-to-down — it is NOT a reliable short-term bounce signal. The edge, such as it is, only shows up by ~6 weeks, past Michael's 1-month window. This cuts against an "exhaustion = immediate liftoff" framing and argues for sizing a starter position and giving it room, not expecting a fast V.

## Concessions (where the bull case is weak)
- **No confirmed bullish RSI divergence.** TDPro's own divergence flag reads 0 (none detected), and my own series check agrees — price and RSI have drifted up together since 10/28 (61.99/RSI 30.25 → 62.08/RSI 30.62), not price-down/RSI-up. This is a basing pattern, not a divergence bottom. Call it a maybe, not a confirmed signal.
- **Every moving average is still sloping down** (EMA8/21/50, SMA50/100/200), and price sits 9-24% below all of them. By the letter of Michael's favorite setup (falling-trendline breakout), this is explicitly NOT that setup yet — the breakout trigger is up at $66-69, not here. Buying now is a bottom-fish on exhaustion signs, not a confirmed trend reversal.
- **Dark pool flow is still net distribution** (-$114M over 7 days) and TDPro flags a "HIGH" alert composite. Under negative gamma, any weak headline (ad-tier guide, hawkish Fed chatter into the Dec 8-9 FOMC) can still air-pocket price fast — the regime that caused the slide doesn't flip until a close clears ~$66.
- **Macro is hostile and NFLX's low correlation cuts both ways.** 0.07 correlation / 0.22 beta to SPX means NFLX mostly trades its own tape — it won't get dragged lower by a broad selloff, but it also won't get a relief-rally tailwind if the market bounces. This is a single-name thesis, not a beta trade.

On the downside, **$60** (max gamma / put wall, round number) is the line that invalidates the basing thesis if closed below.
