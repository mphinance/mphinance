## EQT — Bear conviction: 6/10

1. **It's a one-month bounce inside a seven-month downtrend, not a breakout.** Monthly closes: 63.64 (Mar) → 60.08 → 54.93 → 53.17 → 53.29 → 54.18 → **48.56 (Sep)** → 55.38 (Oct). The "200SMA reclaim" is one green month after a 24% top-to-bottom slide. Dossier's own ADX is 15 — there is no trend by the indicator's own standard, bull or bear.
2. **TP1 sits directly under a stacked resistance wall.** GEX ladder shows Major #2 call wall at $57.50 (netGex +$2.13M, STRONG alignment) — that's *exactly* TP1. Minor resistance also papers 56/57/58/59/60. Price has to clear five labeled strikes to reach a target that's priced as "major resistance."
3. **Spot is barely above the gamma flip (54.39–55.15), a 1-3% cushion.** A garden-variety red day flips the regime from positive (dampened/mean-reverting) to negative (amplified) gamma, right as price nears the stop.
4. **The whole setup is noise relative to the options market's own 1-month expected move.** 1-sigma band for the next month is $50–61 (9.2% implied move) — both the stop ($52.75) and TP1 ($57.50) sit inside a move the market itself calls unremarkable (62% of past 1-month windows this size were "enough," meaning 38% weren't — this isn't a tight, high-conviction range). No dark-pool block prints in the last 10 days (0 prints, not a data failure) — nothing confirms institutional buying under the bounce.
5. **Concession:** quality score 87, IV rank only 38.5 (not rich), and the stock genuinely did clear its 200SMA with Q3 numbers already out and digested (10/27, no more Nov catalyst risk from earnings). The RRC all-stock merger (0.987 ratio, closing Q1 2027) also means a chunk of any further EQT move into year-end is arbitrage-driven, not a directional call on gas — which cuts the bear case too (less discretionary selling pressure, more mechanical arb flow).

**Level that breaks it:** daily close back below $53.20 (50SMA) negates the reclaim and reopens the gap to the September lows near $48.56.

## TXN — Bear conviction: 7/10

1. **This is a chase, not a pullback entry.** TXN is +5.67% in 5 sessions on 1.81x average volume — a vertical move. The stated entry zone (292–300, mid 296) is *at or above* Friday's close (300.84), meaning there's no actual discount being offered; you're being asked to buy the top of a spike and call it "the entry zone."
2. **Two call walls stacked directly between spot and TP1/TP2.** $310 (Major #1, netGex +$3.71M, STRONG) and $315 (Major #2, +$3.19M) sit right before TP1 ($311.50). TP2 ($330) is *above all visible GEX structure* — there's no dealer footprint mapped that high, meaning it requires travelling into unhedged air after already clearing two major walls in under a month.
3. **Options flow does not confirm the rally.** Near-term (11/6) put/call ratio is 2.12 — puts outnumber calls better than 2:1 even as price grinds to new highs. That's a real divergence: the tape is hedging/fading the move, not buying it.
4. **RSI cooling from an extreme (71.8 Thu → 61 today) is typically consolidation-to-down, not a launch pad**, especially one week after the sector took a $1T, Intel-led chip gut-punch that knocked TXN down 3.9% in a single session. TXN-NVDA correlation (0.51/0.49) means it inherits NVDA's Nov-17-earnings/negative-gamma risk with no offsetting TXN-specific catalyst (no Nov earnings of its own).
5. **Concession:** fundamentals are clean — Q3 beat 10/21, all MAs rising, 17% above the 200-day, IV rank only 30 (premium isn't expensive). This isn't a broken chart, it's a bad entry point into resistance with a bearish-skewed near-term options tape.

**Level that breaks it:** close back below $288.9 (21EMA) un-does the breakout attempt and opens room to the 274–278 zone (50SMA / ladder gamma flip).

## USB — Bear conviction: 7/10

1. **The dossier's own language concedes the problem: "50SMA 59.57 (lid)."** The entry zone (58.75–59.50) sits *under a level the plan itself calls a ceiling*, and that ceiling is roughly where spot already is (59.45).
2. **Stacked resistance between entry and both targets.** Primary resistance strike $62.50 (vexRelative 100, STRONG) is also the dominant Apex level and sits exactly at TP2. The $60 major call wall sits right below it. Both targets require clearing resistance that GEX marks as the single strongest strike on the board.
3. **The stop is inside one ordinary week of implied movement.** 1-week 1-sigma band is $57–62; the stop ($57.00) sits right at the bottom edge of that band, and the band's own 68%-hold rate (73%) means ~27% of past weeks this size weren't contained — a non-trending chop can tag this stop with zero thesis change.
4. **Most bearish put/call reading of the five names: 2.52**, sourced from open interest (a standing positioning fact, not a one-day volume blip) — real hedging stacked against a name whose own trade plan has the weakest R:R of the group (0.9 to TP1), such that the "good" outcome requires the TP2 stretch ($65.40) — a fresh 2-month+ high with no nearby technical precedent, sitting barely above the 60-day high of $65.47.
5. **Concession:** quality score 81, cheapest IV rank of the five (24, genuinely cheap premium), and USB is close to uncorrelated to everything else in the book (good diversifier if you must own it). The Friday drop was sector-wide rate repricing, not USB-specific, and the next real catalyst (FOMC) isn't until Dec 8-9 — so this is a low-energy, low-conviction mistake, not a high-conviction one.

**Level that breaks it:** close back below $57.20 (gamma flip / 200SMA 57.17, essentially the stop itself) ends the "cheap vol, buy the dip" case entirely.

## NVDA — Bear conviction: 8/10

1. **Negative gamma regime amplifies moves — it does not mean-revert.** Spot (204.64) is below the flip (210.35–210.54). The regime description is explicit: "price can accelerate quickly as dealers must chase momentum." RSI 35 / Williams %R -87 reads as "oversold, due for a bounce" only if you assume mean reversion — which this gamma regime specifically argues against. The $200 strike is a **-$125M net GEX put wall**, a magnet for volatility, not a floor.
2. **The stop is tighter than one week of normal implied movement.** 1-week 1-sigma band: $193–217. The stop ($194) sits *inside* that band by $1 — a single ordinary week (37% of past weeks this size weren't contained, per the 63% hold rate) can take this out with the actual multi-week thesis never being tested.
3. **Earnings (11/17, confirmed) sit squarely inside the one-month hold.** 1-month implied move is 13% (band $179–234) — nearly double the distance from entry-mid ($202.50) to TP1 ($218.40). This is a scheduled, binary, gap-risk event the stop/TP structure cannot defend against.
4. **Options flow is genuinely split, not quietly bullish.** Session directional-flow conviction came back 0.02 ("balanced" — noise). Digging into the 10/30 unusual-activity tape: $172M bullish premium vs $92M bearish is a net-bullish headline, but several of the single *largest* prints of the day were tagged bearish — a $23.7M bearish-leaning block, a $7.8M bearish call-close, multiple bearish golden sweeps on both calls and puts. There is no institutional consensus here to lean on.
5. **10-day dark pool is net distribution, not accumulation: -$1.4B net notional** ($2.97B sold vs $1.56B bought, off-exchange). That's the opposite of a "smart money is quietly buying the dip" story. Fell 4.2% Friday with the broader semis selloff — it's trading as sector beta, not an idiosyncratic value name finding a floor.
6. **Concession:** quality score 99 — as good as fundamentals get in this list — and sitting on the 200SMA with RSI 35 is a textbook setup *for a trader who believes in mean reversion*. If NVDA holds $200 on close, the bull case is real. This is the highest-quality name on the list; the bear case here is about regime mechanics and timing, not the business.

**Level that breaks it:** a daily close below $200 likely triggers the negative-gamma acceleration mechanism straight toward $195 (major put support) and the 60-day low, blowing through the $194 stop on a gap rather than a controlled test.

## HIMS — Bear conviction: 9/10 (strongest bear case of the five)

1. **The trade plan as written is barely executable.** Stop ($25.60) is ~7.5% below provisional entry-mid ($27.10). Earnings land 11/9 AMC, inside week 2 of the hold, and the options market is pricing a **±16% one-week move and ±22% two-week move** around that date (dossier's own figure: last print moved -17% in two days). An 8% stop sized against an event the market is pricing at double-to-triple that size isn't a stop, it's a coin flip that resolves on the print, not on the "swing" thesis the R:R table (1.9/4.6) is framed around.
2. **Dealer structure leans down, not up.** Negative gamma below flip (28.44); dominant/call-wall resistance sits at $30 (above spot); two put-support walls stacked underneath at $26 and $25. The $200-style "floor" language doesn't apply here — the nearest big strike above is resistance, not a magnet pulling price up.
3. **It's sitting on its 200SMA (27.03), not reclaiming it — current price (27.68) is basically on top of the average with no separation**, after a two-year-plus comedown from $34.67 (June) with progressively lower highs into the coil. RSI 44.9 (below midline) — this isn't a name showing strength into its binary event.
4. **What little options flow exists skews bearish into earnings.** Only 3 unusual prints on 10/30 (small sample, concede that), but the two largest were both "Bearish (Calls Closing)" — institutional holders taking profit/de-risking calls ahead of the print, not building fresh upside.
5. **Unresolved overhang:** CAO departed 10/9, unexplained; "entry zone" is explicitly provisional, meaning there is no real trade plan yet, just a placeholder waiting on a binary event. Fell 6.7% Friday — the highest-beta name on the list (2.8x SPY) took the worst hit on the worst sector day.
6. **Concession:** IV rank is only "mid" (48.8), not screaming expensive given realized vol, short interest (53.5M sh, 5 days to cover) is real squeeze fuel if the print goes the other way, and the Novo GLP-1 suit settling in March removes one tail risk. If you want to buy HIMS as an explicit earnings-binary bet with a stop sized for a gap, that's a defensible (different) trade. It is not the "pullback into support, hold for a month" trade this entry zone describes.

**Level that breaks it:** close below $26.33 (20-day low) or the $25 major put wall confirms the coil is resolving down — and it will most likely happen via the earnings gap itself, before Thanksgiving, not via a slow grind.
