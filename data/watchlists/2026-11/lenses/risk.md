## Portfolio view (all four at stated sizes)

- Total stated risk if every stop fills at its level: 0.5R + 0.25R + 0.25R + 0.25R = 1.25R. Gap risk (TSLA especially) and any entry that does not fill at the trigger can push the realized figure above that.
- Beta-weighted (63-day betas, my heuristic): 0.5 x 2.16 + 0.25 x (0.76 + 1.73 + 0.41) = 1.08 + 0.73, about 1.8 R-equivalent of SPY exposure. The book is net long market beta in a risk-off tape, with TSLA contributing 60% of it.
- Realized correlations are low (126-day daily returns): TSLA/PCVX 0.18, HUM/PCVX 0.23, TSLA/HUM 0.10, FNF about 0 against the others. So there is no pair that is a duplicate position in normal times.
- Correlated cluster in a selloff: TSLA + PCVX are the two high-beta, long-duration-equity names (beta 2.16 and 1.73); in the 2025 selloff they fell 38.5% and 63.8%. Do not hold both at full size. HUM and FNF are low beta but both fell harder than SPY in the 2026 selloff, so they are not a hedge for the other two.
- Pair that should not be held together: TSLA + PCVX. If one slot only, TSLA is the only one with a structured plan.
- Fewer-risk alternative: hold TSLA at 0.5R only if the dip fills and the daily close is back at or above 352; take nothing else. That caps stated risk at 0.5R.
